# Decision summary

Recap only. Draws on the discussion and the decision doc, adds no new ideas, never edits the decision doc, and does not advance the workflow. Saves nothing unless asked. The discussion can continue afterwards.

Rules:
- A gap you must fill is marked as an assumption.
- If the latest discussion and the decision doc disagree, flag the conflict; do not pick one.
- Plain words, unavoidable technical terms explained, concrete numbers, no invented shorthand.

Sections (omit empty ones):
1. **Needs you:** open questions or decisions only the user can make.
2. **What you'll get:** the functional outcome, what changes, what stays the same.
3. **How it works:** main pieces, how they connect, what is new, reused, removed.
4. **What it costs:** effort, risk, trade-offs.
5. **What we ruled out:** options rejected and why.
6. **Changed along the way:** differences from the original ask.
